Braided P

The letter P drawn as a cornrow, finished with a gold bead. Instantly says “braider”, and the P works alone as the Instagram avatar.

06 · Heritage

Duafe comb

Based on Duafe, the West African Adinkra symbol of the wooden comb: beauty, care and femininity. Kente-style stripe underneath. Meaningful and timeless.

07 · Crown

Golden afro

A profile silhouette with a gold afro crown and hoop earring. The most recognisable, popular style for African salons; strong on signage and merch.

08 · Plait

Single braid

One three-strand plait ending in a bead, in gold and terracotta. The most minimal of the set; feels like a luxury label.
