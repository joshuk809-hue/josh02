Precious: four logo directions

Each shown on dark olive and on cream. Pick one, or mix parts (e.g. mark from 2, wordmark from 4).

01 · Botanical

Leaf monogram

A serif “P” with a sprouting leaf, the closest to the Luméra reference. Calm, natural, premium; works for skincare add-ons later.

02 · Gem

Precious stone comb

A cut gemstone whose lower facets become comb teeth: “precious” plus hair in one mark. Bold and memorable as an app icon.

03 · Portrait

Flowing curl

A fine-line profile whose hair sweeps back into a soft curl. Elegant and personal; reads clearly as a hair salon.

04 · Editorial

Scissor seal

An italic wordmark inside a round seal, with open scissors forming the top. Boutique, works well on stickers, cards and Instagram.
