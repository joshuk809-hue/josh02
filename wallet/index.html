Hamburgheria del Contadino · Carta Fedeltà

Non si mostra. Si avvicina.

Three contactless fidelity cards that live in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Customers hold their phone to the reader, as if paying, and the points arrive on their own. The card grows with them, from the first sprout to the Riserva set aside for the best guests.

In the wallet, senza codici.

No barcode to scan: each pass carries its card number over NFC. Press "Prova il tap" to see a visit being recorded.

Contactless on Apple Wallet

  1. Create a Pass Type ID in an Apple Developer account, then ask Apple to enable NFC for it (Apple approves loyalty programmes case by case).
  2. Generate a P-256 key pair for NFC. The public key goes in APPLE_NFC_PUBLIC_KEY, and the private key goes into the reader.
  3. Build and sign the passes: node tools/build-wallet-passes.js with the APPLE_* variables set.
  4. The till needs a reader that supports Apple VAS. Many current payment terminals already do.

Contactless on Google Wallet

  1. Get an Issuer ID in the Google Pay & Wallet Console, and ask for Smart Tap to be enabled.
  2. Set GOOGLE_SMART_TAP=1 together with the GOOGLE_* variables, then build. Each customer gets an "Add to Google Wallet" link.
  3. The pass sends the card number to the reader on tap (smartTapRedemptionValue).
  4. The till needs a Smart Tap-certified reader.

The passes contain no QR code: the card number travels only through the contactless chip in the phone. Until NFC is enabled, customers can still read their card number on the back of the pass.
